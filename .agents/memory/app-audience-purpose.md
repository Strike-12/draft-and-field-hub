---
name: App audience and purpose
description: The intended audience and primary use cases for Draft & Field Hub.
---

The app is for people who want to build a fantasy team in the app, check player stats, and see recent performance. Connecting an existing ESPN league is optional.

**Why:** The user explicitly described the intended audience and use cases.

**How to apply:** Keep team building and player research useful without an ESPN league connection; treat synced rosters as optional.
